A Brief, Unnecessary History

The <marquee> tag was Internet Explorer's answer to Netscape's <blink> — introduced in 1996, it made text scroll horizontally across the screen. It was never part of any HTML standard, was deprecated for over a decade, and yet here it lives on, reincarnated in pure CSS @keyframes, immune to deprecation forever.
