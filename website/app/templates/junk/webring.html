The AstronautMarkus Junk Webring

A webring is a collection of websites linked together in a circle, each one pointing to the "next" and "previous" site so visitors could ring-hop between related pages without ever using a search engine. This one is delightfully self-contained: it only links between the junk pages on this very site, forming a perfect little ouroboros of nostalgia. Every page below carries the ring widget at the bottom — click Prev/Next/Random from any of them and you'll stay inside the ring forever.
