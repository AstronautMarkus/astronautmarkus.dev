What Was the Y2K Bug?

Back when storage was expensive, programmers stored years using only two digits (like "99" instead of "1999") to save space. When the clock rolled over to the year 2000, systems would in theory read "00" as 1900, breaking calculations everywhere. Billions were spent fixing it. The clock rolled over. Mostly nothing happened. This tracker exists to honor that anticlimax.
