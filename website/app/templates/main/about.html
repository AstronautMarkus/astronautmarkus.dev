▶ About Me

▶ Fun Facts
Banned from School Computers... then admin In primary school, I was banned from using the school computers for writing a simple .vbs script that shut them down. Now I'm the admin of the school's network and servers — and I'm the one banning students who try to hack the school computers.
Bye bye Windows, Hello Unix In 2024, midway through my degree, after a tense situation and a failed first attempt with Ubuntu , I gave it another shot — but this time with more thought. The change was radical: the optimization, the experience, everything. I was learning it all — the terminal, commands, the filesystem, the philosophy behind it. I finally understood what a kernel really is, what Unix is, the difference between Linux and Windows NT, what GNU/Linux means, and more. I was learning, and I was loving it. I was finally free from the chains of Windows, and I was ready to embrace the power of Unix.
Then Linux, Now Darwin Linux is unique, great, and powerful, but I wanted to try something new, something different. Philosophy matters, but you also need to get things done at work, so I decided to buy an Intel MacBook Air 2017, the A1466 — and I was amazed: the experience, the optimization, the performance, the battery life, everything. Then I bought a Mac Mini M1, and I was amazed again: the experience, the optimization, the performance, the energy efficiency, everything. Finally, I bought a MacBook Neo, the "iPhone" of laptops, and I was amazed all over again. For day-to-day use, I'd definitely go with a Mac; it's second to none for work. But for the real me — the sysadmin, the hacker, the tinkerer, the one who loves to explore and learn — Linux is still my first love. I'll always have a special place in my heart for both Linux and Darwin. By the way, who the hell is this "Windows" guy?
