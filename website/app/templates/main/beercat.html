What is BeerCat?

BeerCat (Informática BeerCat) is my own IT services company, based in Chile. It started in 2025 as Reyes&Friends, building custom software for local clients, and in 2026 became Informática BeerCat — offering the same quality of service to businesses of any size, without the complications.

No two clients get the same cookie-cutter solution — every project is scoped, quoted in writing, and built around what the business actually needs.
