Performance

This site is built on the same philosophy mentioned on the About page: stay under 5MB, avoid frameworks that ship megabytes of JavaScript for a blog, and actually measure things instead of assuming. Most of the pages below pull real numbers from this exact request — a few are jokes, and are labeled as such.
